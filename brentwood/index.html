How Brentwood Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year if their water is hard.

The age of your heater plays a big role. With regular service, a tank water heater typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, popping noises, or a damp spot under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or thermal expansion with nowhere to go, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can wear out the valve and stress the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Common Water Heater Problems in Brentwood

In Brentwood, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units lose elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
