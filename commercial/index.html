Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a busy dinner service, and an undersized heater falls behind right when the dish room backs up. Franklin Water Heater handles food-service hot water, including high-input gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, proper sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a core issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in tanks stored too cool, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Franklin Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Franklin and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore hot water, and document the entire call for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

A maintenance program from Franklin Water Heater gets built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include anode checks and tank flushing, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the tap. Hard water shortens the life of commercial equipment quickly, so busy properties usually need quarterly visits.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
