Warning Signs to Watch For

A heater that is starting to struggle rarely hides it for long, and the clues are easy to spot once you know them. Keep an eye out for the following:

  • Fixtures that run too hot one minute and cool the next
  • Long waits for hot water at sinks far from the heater
  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging from a heavily used tank
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • The tank giving up in the middle of a rush
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life

Tips to Keep Your Water Heater Healthy

A few simple habits, like an annual flush and a quick look at the relief valve, can add years to a tank. Here's where to begin:

  • Track temperatures at the dish machine and hand sinks as part of routine checks
  • Keep recirculation pumps and check valves in service to avoid long waits and wasted energy
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Replace consumed anode rods before the tank itself starts to corrode
  • Have mixing valves checked so fixtures stay neither scalding nor lukewarm
  • Flush commercial tanks more often than residential ones, often every few months in heavy use or hard water
  • Plan replacement before an aging unit fails during peak season
